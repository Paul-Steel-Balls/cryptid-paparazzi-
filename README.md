# Cryptid Paparazzi

Photograph cryptids for the tabloid. Meet the quota, don't get caught.

Godot 4.7.2, Forward+ renderer, built for Windows PC.

## Download and play (Windows)
1. Download **[CryptidPaparazzi-0.12-windows.zip](https://github.com/Paul-Steel-Balls/cryptid-paparazzi-/raw/main/download/CryptidPaparazzi-0.12-windows.zip)** (73 MB).
2. Unzip it and run `CryptidPaparazzi-0.12.exe`. It's one file with everything inside. Windows SmartScreen may warn because it isn't code-signed: click "More info", then "Run anyway".
3. To play together, see [Multiplayer](#multiplayer-co-op-up-to-4) below.

## Run from source
Open this folder in Godot 4.7.2 (it imports on first open) and press Play, or run `./build.sh 0.12` to run the tests and export a Windows `.exe`.

## Where this came from

The source was recovered from `CryptidPaparazzi_2.apk`, version 0.2, package `com.sophia.cryptidpaparazzi`:
- The compiled GDScript (`.gdc`, tokenizer v101) was detokenized back to `.gd`. Line numbers match the original. Comments didn't survive the original export, which is why there are blank gaps.
- The binary scenes (`.scn`) were converted to text `.tscn` using Godot 4.7.2 itself.
- `project.godot` was rebuilt from `project.binary`.
- Check: the game's own test suite passes 64/64, and the main scene runs with no errors.

## Controls

| Action | PC |
|---|---|
| Move / sprint | WASD / Shift (sprinting makes noise; can be set to tap-to-toggle in the pause menu) |
| Look | Mouse |
| Raise the camera to your eye (always first person, even from third person) | Hold right mouse button |
| Snap a photo (only while raised; 5 s flash recharge) | Left click |
| Jump | Space |
| Crouch (slower, quieter, harder to spot) | C toggles, or hold Ctrl |
| Pick bait / trap / tripod cam / joint / beer | 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 / 5 |
| Use what's picked | Q |
| 1st / 3rd person | V (outdoors only) |
| Talk / enter / use | E |
| Break out of a UFO's tractor beam | Mash A and D (alternating) |
| Pause, settings, Cryptid Files, Multiplayer | Esc |
| Fullscreen | F11 or Alt+Enter (or the pause menu) |
| Microphone (optional, off by default) | Pause menu → Microphone. Cryptids hear you talk: a whisper carries ~8 m, a yell ~35 m |

## How a run works
- Every map has its own cast of cryptids, defined in `scripts/species.gd`. Commons show up every night; uncommons, rares and legendaries roll each night.
- Hold right-click to raise the camera, left-click to shoot. Film count shows on the camera.
- Go into the **RV** (E at the door): the **darkroom** develops your film (money + album + Cryptid Files), the **bed** ends the night, the **driver's seat** travels to unlocked maps. Undeveloped photos keep overnight and on the road.
- **Toe Rogan** at the campfire: his WANTED poster shows who's on this map. He swaps your camera for a fresh one (film reload). Show him a developed photo of every common and uncommon on the map and he unlocks the next one.
- The **stand** next to the RV sells bait ($100), traps ($200), tripod cams ($1,500), joints ($400) and beer ($150). You can carry 5 of each. Photos taken while drunk or high pay half.
- Get photographed by grey tourists three times in one night and a **UFO** comes for you. Snap it before it reaches you for a fortune; if the beam gets you, mash A/D to break free, or lose your camera and undeveloped film.
- The **Leperchaun**'s pot of gold is out in the Deep Woods. Find it unguarded and it's $150 cash, once a night. He will chase you.
- About one night in 365 (and every 365th night), **Santa** parks on your RV roof.
- Grey tourists are faster than you and their flash blinds you for a couple of seconds.
- If a hunter grabs you, the night ends and your undeveloped photos are gone.
- Progress saves automatically (`user://cryptid_save.json`); settings separately (`user://cryptid_settings.json`).

## Multiplayer (co-op, up to 4)
- **Host**: Esc → MULTIPLAYER → type your name → HOST A GAME ON THIS PC. Your PC is the server: your world (cryptids, the night, the map) is the one everyone plays in. Windows will ask to let the game through the firewall the first time: allow it.
- **Join**: Esc → MULTIPLAYER → type the host's address → JOIN.
  - Same house: use the address the host's panel shows (e.g. 192.168.1.20).
  - Over the internet: the host either forwards **UDP port 24680** on their router to their PC and gives friends their public IP, or everyone installs a free VPN like Tailscale and joins with the host's Tailscale IP (no router setup).
- Only the host can sleep or drive; everyone follows. Money, photos, album and unlocks are each player's own.
- Online, getting grabbed knocks you out (you lose your undeveloped film and come to at camp) instead of ending the night. The joint just makes you high (no slow-mo: the cryptids are shared).
- Not shared yet: tripod cams, the UFO (each player gets their own), City pedestrians. No voice chat yet.
- Command line: `CryptidPaparazzi.exe -- --host`, `-- --join=1.2.3.4`, `-- --name=Ian`.

## Adding a map
1. `scripts/species.gd` → `MAPS`: name, ambience, `where` (how Toe talks about it, e.g. "up on this mountain") and the roster by tier.
2. `scripts/species.gd` → `STYLES`: how it looks (terrain type, seed, tree/bush/fern/flower counts, colours, sky, fog, fireflies). Anything left out uses `STYLE_DEFAULT`.
3. Add its id to `MAP_ORDER`. Toe unlocks maps in that order.
4. Cryptids with a `home` (Methman's camp, the Jersey Devil's tailgate, the Leperchaun's gold) get their spot on any map they live on. The City places them itself (`scripts/city.gd` `landmarks`).
